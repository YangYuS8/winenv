# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our standard

Winenv should be a place where people can improve software together without harassment, intimidation, or gatekeeping. Contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to:

- discuss ideas and code with respect, even when they disagree;
- criticize behavior and technical decisions rather than people;
- welcome questions from people with different experience, language, identity, and background;
- give actionable feedback and accept correction in good faith;
- protect private information and avoid publishing another person's logs or identity without permission;
- prioritize community safety over winning an argument.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate misgendering, stalking, doxxing, sustained disruption, and retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions about Winenv elsewhere, and public spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Do not open a public issue about sensitive conduct. Contact the maintainer privately using a contact method listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/YangYuS8). Include relevant links, dates, and a factual description; share only the minimum private material needed to investigate.

If no private contact method is available, avoid publishing identifying details and use GitHub's platform reporting tools for urgent abuse. Security vulnerabilities follow the separate [security policy](./SECURITY.md).

Reports will be considered as privately as practical. The maintainer will disclose information only when needed to investigate, protect participants, or meet a legal obligation. Knowingly false reports or retaliation are themselves violations.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and history, the maintainer may issue a private correction, remove content, limit participation, temporarily block an account, or permanently exclude someone from project spaces. Immediate threats, doxxing, or severe harassment may skip intermediate steps.

Decisions should be documented privately with the behavior, evidence, action, and review date. A person subject to action may request one reconsideration by providing material context that was not previously available. The maintainer's final decision governs project spaces.

This project also endorses the principles of [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/); this repository-specific policy controls its reporting and enforcement process.
